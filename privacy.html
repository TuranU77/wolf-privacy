Privacy Policy for WOLF – Party Game

Updated: October 25, 2026 · WOLF 2.0

In brief: Multiplayer does not require an email address or password. You can use a nickname of your choice. To let your devices play together, our service provider Supabase stores the necessary game information and technical identifiers. These are used to operate the game, not for personalized advertising.

1. Controller and contact

Turan Usuk is responsible for the processing of data in WOLF. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact design@usuk.de.

2. Local play

WOLF offers local games and optional multiplayer. In local games, entered player names, roles, settings and game saves are processed on your device. Local gameplay does not require an internet connection. Purchases, purchase restoration and opening external links may separately connect to external services.

3. Multiplayer and Supabase

When you create or join a multiplayer game, Supabase connects the devices to the same room and keeps the shared game state in sync. The service creates a technical user identifier in the background for this purpose. No registration with an email address or password is required. The identifier and your nickname link information to your game session, so this data is pseudonymous rather than fully anonymous.

The following information is transmitted to and stored by Supabase for your shared game:

  • Your chosen player name or nickname;
  • technical user, participant and room identifiers and the room code;
  • room membership, readiness status and timestamps;
  • game configuration, role assignments and state required for shared gameplay.

Please use a nickname rather than your full name where possible. The app stores authentication tokens in the device Keychain to reuse your session. Network connections also involve your IP address; service providers may process technical access and security logs.

Other participants see names and status information intended for the shared game. The moderator receives information needed to run the game, including roles. Secret roles are not intended to be a public participant list.

The provider is Supabase Pte. Ltd. Supabase processes submitted multiplayer data as a processor. Subprocessors and processing outside the EU/EEA may be involved. Information about contractual safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses, is available in the Supabase Data Processing Addendum. Additional provider information is available at Supabase Privacy.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Processing required for a game you choose to use serves provision of the app and multiplayer functionality (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Technical security processing and general inquiries serve our legitimate interests in secure operation and communication with users (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Legally required retention is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. Online participation is not possible without the necessary multiplayer data; local games remain an alternative.

5. Retention and deletion

Local settings and game saves are stored to support app use and game resumption. Technical authentication data in the Keychain may survive reinstallation.

Ending a game, leaving a room or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete all associated server data. This policy does not promise automatic deletion of multiplayer data after a fixed number of hours. Necessary retention depends on providing games and reconnection, resolving errors and security incidents, and any legal obligations. Data no longer required must be deleted; backups and technical logs may follow separate provider retention cycles.

For access or deletion requests, email design@usuk.de. Where possible, include your nickname, room code and approximate game time so we can locate the data and verify your entitlement. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

6. Camera access for QR codes

With camera permission, WOLF reads a room QR code on your device. The app does not upload camera images to the multiplayer server. You can enter the room code manually instead. Camera permission can be withdrawn in iOS Settings.

7. In-app purchases

Purchases and restoration use Apple StoreKit. The app processes product and transaction information to unlock purchased content. We do not receive credit card or bank details through this process. Apple's own processing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

8. Advertising, tracking and support

WOLF does not use advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs. Multiplayer data is used to operate games, not for personalized advertising. If you contact us by email, we process your email address and message to handle your inquiry.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable legal conditions, you have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests for reasons related to your particular situation. You may withdraw consent with future effect where processing relies on consent. You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

10. This website and updates

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit the page, GitHub processes technically necessary access data, such as your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We update this policy when the app or its data processing changes.
